💫 About Me
Hey, I'm Kevin, a developer at Elaria who loves turning ideas into polished digital products.
At Elaria I own mobile releases: getting our apps built, signed and shipped to the App Store and Google Play. Next, I'll be helping out on the backend in Go.
My home turf is React Native, Expo and modern web stacks like Next.js. I like clean UI, fast performance and building tools people actually use.
